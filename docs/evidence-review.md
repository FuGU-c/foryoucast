## Readiness limits

- These checks establish source traceability for three examples, not full-catalog accuracy.
- `story-1` and `story-6` have no spoken figures in the selected turns; only `story-5` supplies the three spoken percentages. The linked article supports the figures and their periods, including September in its fourth-paragraph table. The official release and answer key remain unchecked, so independent verification is still outstanding.
- Each selected demo story has one primary `claim_source_id`. Related sources in a story are not automatically evidence for every sentence, and the current summaries do not document disagreements between outlets.
- This fixed sample review does not establish that generated editions have the same source mapping or accuracy. The live Podcast Studio smoke passed its source-quote review, and one Medium edition requested at approximately five minutes completed with four natural chapters. Its measured duration was 265.04 seconds (4:25), with source review passed for all four chapters. These workflow checks do not replace a human source check or prove independent truth. Listen to the generated edition and follow its claims before making an accuracy claim.

The dataset terms allow source links and short quotations but forbid republishing article text. This note therefore records IDs, links, paraphrased checks and limitations only.
