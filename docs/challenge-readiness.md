# ForYouCast judging readiness

**Audit date:** 21 November 2026  
**Scope:** ForYouCast track brief, supplied dataset guidance and licence, current app, local measurements, and available cloud smoke artifacts.

## Read this first

The demo has four task-style listener accounts, six prerecorded editions, a 1,740-article replay catalog, and four published n8n workflows. A Podcast Studio smoke run, one-article News Desk smoke run, short ElevenLabs narration run, and a Medium natural-voice asynchronous request configured for approximately five minutes have passed. The Medium request reached `ready` at 100%, contains four ready natural-audio chapters, and measured 265.04 seconds (4:25). Byte-range playback was verified.

The most important remaining judging gaps are evidence quality and scope. Three prerecorded summaries have been reviewed against their linked article paragraphs; the retail-sales article supports the October and September labels in its paragraph-four table, but the official release and answer key have not been checked. The News Desk has passed on one report; cross-outlet grouping across all 1,740 inputs has not been validated against the answer key. Model-token cost is projected from one sample article, while ElevenLabs USD, n8n executions, hosting, and a complete approximately five-minute natural-audio cost remain unknown.

## Feature-specific audit

- **Profiles and languages:** The PDF p. 1 requires visibly different briefings and a live wildcard test, but does not prescribe detailed profile cards or particular narration languages. The ZIP `README.txt`, “WHAT IS IN HERE,” describes source articles in English, German, and Dutch. The app provides those three summary/narration languages and role-based examples based on optional ideas; compare two listeners on the same replay coverage. The wildcard still needs its live profile.
- **Source provenance:** The PDF p. 1 requires every spoken claim to trace to its source article. The app keeps source IDs and publisher links with stories and checks generated quotes against supplied paragraphs. These checks establish provenance to the submitted reporting; they do not independently verify that reporting or check it against official releases/the answer key.
- **“Since you last listened”:** This per-profile history view uses the dataset replay date and stored reading/listening history. It is a useful product feature, not a separately named PDF deliverable and not a live-news update. For the demo, create a history event, reopen the profile, and show the replay-date delta; explain that an empty state can mean the profile has no prior history.
- **Duplicate and event grouping:** The local catalog contains 1,459 story records and 238 translation links, with 108 records having multiple linked sources. Those counts do not show that the right duplicates or events were merged. The live News Desk can suggest `same-event` or `translation`, but its one-report smoke was classified as `new`; answer-key validation across feeds remains open.
- **Scale and spend:** The local timings cover 1,740 records; the 17,400-row tag pass repeats those same records ten times. The cloud token projection uses one observed News Desk report per article and two Studio editions requested at approximately five minutes. Neither figure is an all-in quote or a 17,400-unique-article cloud benchmark. See [`performance-measurements.json`](performance-measurements.json) and [`cost-model.md`](cost-model.md).

## Demo runbook

1. Compare two task-style profiles on the same replay date. Show changed story choice, order, depth, and narration language.
2. Follow a spoken chapter to its source links. State that the workflow checks source traceability; the retail-sales sample is supported by its source article, but the official release and answer key have not been checked.
3. Show one reviewed multi-source catalog story, then distinguish that prepared example from a verified full-corpus clustering result.
4. Use News Desk as a one-report live preparation example. State that the smoke passed with three claims and three summary languages, and that corpus-wide grouping is not yet validated.
5. Play the completed Medium Natural edition requested at approximately five minutes and show its saved ready state. This run measured 4:25 (265.04 seconds); do not generalize that duration to future requests. Use a prerecorded sample as the fallback.
6. Present the 1,740 and 17,400 cost scenarios from `cost-model.md`; separate model-token estimates from unknown audio, hosting, and workflow charges.
7. Explain who pays, how a newsroom uses the briefing, how synthetic speech is disclosed, and what the dataset licence permits.

## GitHub and dataset rights

The supplied ZIP's `README.txt` prohibits republishing article text, sharing the files beyond the team, and retaining dataset copies after the event. The current `.gitignore` excludes `data/`, `.secrets/`, `*.zip`, and generated media. Still inspect the staged file list before a later upload: ignored files can be force-added, and generated scripts/audio may be event-derived. Ask ForYouCast whether those derivatives and the reproduced mark may be reused publicly; remove them unless permission is explicit. No upload is part of this audit.
