# ForYouCast presentation video — 2 minutes 30 seconds

The story is one journey: **why it matters → who it serves → personalization → discovery → trust → memory → preparation → listening → ownership → customization → product path → close.** Related controls are grouped so the video feels like a product story rather than a list of menus.

**Runtime:** exactly 150 seconds including opening, transitions, product-audio excerpt and end card. **Delivery:** 1920×1080, 30 fps (4,500 frames). **Narration:** one consistent English presenter, warm and confident, approximately 130–145 words/minute. The podcast itself supplies the two-host example. Never play both narration tracks together.

The transcript is provided per scene so ElevenLabs timing can be fitted without stretching the whole performance. Durations are editing slots, not a guarantee of generated speech length. Trim leading/trailing silence, keep natural breaths, and pad holds inside each slot; rewrite a line if needed rather than rushing or cutting words.
